Privacy Policy

La Veranda Restaurant Call Assistant
Effective date: August 22, 2026

La Veranda Restaurant Call Assistant is an Android application for authorized La Veranda staff. It helps staff manage restaurant calls, reservation records, calendar events, and daily reservation briefings.

Information the app accesses

Depending on permissions granted by the user, the app may access phone state, incoming phone numbers, call-log information, contacts, and calendar events. The app also processes reservation details entered by staff, such as customer names, phone numbers, reservation dates and times, party details, preferences, and notes.

How information is used

This information is used to identify eligible restaurant calls, display the reservation form, store reservation records, create and display events in the calendar selected by the user, and generate daily PDF reservation briefings.

Google and Gmail data

The app requests the Google OAuth scope https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.send. This permission is used only to send a briefing email and PDF attachment through the Gmail account explicitly connected by the user, to recipients selected by the user.

The app does not use Gmail access to read the inbox, read existing messages, modify or delete messages, access Gmail contacts, advertise, or sell information. The app does not request or store the user's Gmail password. Google handles authentication and authorization, and the app does not permanently store the Gmail access token.

La Veranda Restaurant Call Assistant's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Calendar data

The app accesses the calendar selected by the user through Android's calendar system. Calendar data is used to create reservation events, display events, and generate the daily briefing. A generated briefing may contain event titles, dates, times, and descriptions.

Storage and sharing

Reservation records, whitelist entries, recipient addresses, briefing settings, schedule settings, and delivery status are stored in the app's private storage on the Android device. Depending on Android backup and device-transfer settings, this local data may be included in a system backup or device transfer.

Temporary PDF files may be created in the app's cache. When a briefing is sent, its content is transmitted to Google's Gmail API and delivered to the recipients selected by the user. Copies may remain in the connected Gmail account's Sent folder and recipients' mailboxes. Calendar information may also be processed by the calendar provider configured on the device.

The app does not sell personal information and does not use it for advertising or marketing profiles.

Retention and deletion

Local information remains on the device until it is deleted in the app, the app's storage is cleared, or the app is uninstalled. Uninstalling the app does not delete calendar events already created, emails already sent, copies delivered to recipients, or information retained in device backups.

Users can remove recipients, disable scheduled delivery, revoke Android permissions in device settings, and revoke Google access through Google Account connections.

Security

Reasonable measures are used to protect information handled by the app, but no device, network transmission, or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Users are responsible for protecting access to their device and connected Google account.

Children's privacy

This application is intended for restaurant business operations and is not directed to children.

Changes

This policy may be updated when the app's features or data-handling practices change. The effective date above will be updated when material changes are made.
